Prerelease assurance

Security findings are visible, fixed, and bounded.

Samsarix LLC completed a standard Codex Security review on August 11, 2026 against revision a2d460e47f5ae2fa1897c6bb38b265a1676f38cb. The review covered the maintained browser registry, CLI, Action, build and Pages workflow, plus runnable Node, Python, Discord, webhook, generator, and deployment prototypes. Git history, live deployment state, binary internals, and most archival prose were outside its scope.

Download the canonical Markdown review record.

Result

The scan reported eight source-backed findings: one high-severity current-tree privacy exposure, five medium-severity readiness-integrity, WebSocket, and legacy DOM issues, and two low-severity export and URL-retention issues.

  • Identified personal conversation, context, workspace-output, Notion-log, and runtime-log exports were removed from the current tree, with filename guards added.
  • Calendar-invalid and future-dated evidence is blocked, and active records need a versioned interface before they can be ready.
  • Credential-like URL values are rejected and Markdown exports encode imported raw HTML.
  • Validated legacy navigation, Zapier WebSocket, generated webhook, and browser-stream paths are authenticated or constrained, bounded, and rendered as text.

Release controls

Pages is HTTPS-enforced and limited to main. The protected branch requires the validate check, pull-request flow, and resolved conversations and blocks force pushes and deletion. Repository administrators retain recovery bypass access.

Residual gates

This review does not certify the product or prove production safety. Stable or commercial promotion still requires:

  • account-side revocation or rotation of historical credential categories;
  • a decision on coordinated Git-history rewriting and cache-removal requests;
  • provenance and redistribution approval for retained binary and data artifacts; and
  • appropriate legal review of the source-available and commercial terms.

Unsupported prototypes need a separate release boundary and security review before deployment.
