Readiness method

Visible evidence, explicit blockers.

The registry scores purpose, ownership, interface, authentication, data handling, evaluation, security, human oversight, and operations. Missing critical evidence overrides a numerical score. Concept, paused, and retired records cannot be labeled ready.

A2A import carries identity, skills, interface, provider, and security-declaration metadata into a review record. It deliberately leaves organization-specific governance evidence missing.
